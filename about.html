About Premier Remodeling

We’re More Than Just Home Remodeling Contractors

We’ve served homeowners across Northern Utah for more than 15 years, completing home renovations and new-home builds with an emphasis on luxury, craftsmanship and customer satisfaction.

Premier Remodeling: A Family-Owned Business

Family-owned, customer-oriented remodeling.

After working in construction for most of his life, our owner, Luke, saw a need for more specialized, customer-oriented remodeling contractors. He and his wife, Nancy, founded Premier Remodeling to help homeowners bring their dream homes to life and renew the homes they already love.

Luke has guided our design and renovation process with the goal of giving homeowners a positive experience throughout their projects. Over time, his brothers Caleb and Matthew and their father Greg joined the team, along with talented trade partners who have become part of the Premier family.

We enjoy challenging, one-of-a-kind projects and place a high priority on listening to customers, addressing concerns and making sure homeowners are satisfied with the finished work.
